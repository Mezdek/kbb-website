## Our purpose

We promote international understanding, tolerance between cultures and religions, and the practice of religion. In particular through:

- maintaining and practising Islamic life at the {{mosqueName}}, especially communal prayer, religious festivals and religious education;
- courses, seminars and talks on language learning, education and integration;
- bringing together and exchanging views between people of different origins and cultures;
- supporting and accompanying refugees and people in need;
- cultural and community events open to everyone.

Our integration, education and outreach offerings are open to all people, regardless of origin, religion or belief.
