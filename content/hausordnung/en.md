# Mosque House Rules

These house rules apply to everyone present in the mosque — members, guests, and children. Entering the building constitutes acceptance of these rules. Violations of these house rules may result in removal from the mosque, a ban from entering, or referral to the relevant authorities (police).

---

1. The instructions of the board and appointed supervisors must be followed.
2. Visits by non-Muslims and guided tours must be arranged with the board in advance.
3. Shoes must be removed before entering the prayer hall and placed in the designated areas.
4. Modest clothing that appropriately covers the body must be worn.
5. Anyone who has eaten garlic or onions and gives off their smell may only enter the prayer hall once the smell has dissipated.
6. Quiet must be maintained throughout the building; mobile phones must be set to silent.
7. Eating and drinking in the prayer hall are prohibited, except on designated occasions.
8. The rooms, sanitary facilities, courtyard, and entrance area must be kept clean; waste must be disposed of in the designated containers.
9. Mosque property must be treated with care; Qurans, prayer rugs, chairs, and other items must be returned to their place after use.
10. The person responsible is liable for damage caused intentionally or through negligence.
11. Sound, heating, and AV systems may only be operated or altered by authorized persons.
12. Smoking in the building, including e-cigarettes, is prohibited.
13. The consumption or bringing of alcohol and drugs is prohibited; persons who are intoxicated or who disturb order and quiet will be denied entry.
14. Bringing animals into the prayer areas is prohibited.
15. Parents and legal guardians are responsible at all times for supervising their children in the mosque.
16. Consideration must be shown toward the neighborhood; disturbances must be avoided, particularly during the Fajr prayer, evening events, and when arriving and leaving.
17. Parking in the mosque's forecourt, in the building's entrance area, or in a manner that obstructs neighboring properties and driveways is prohibited.
18. Use of the rooms for events (funerals, weddings, classes, etc.) requires prior approval from the board.
19. Selling, advertising, and collecting donations in the mosque are prohibited without approval.
20. Any political activity, extremist propaganda, and hate speech are prohibited, as is the dissemination of content or behavior that incites hatred or violence.
21. Photography as well as audio and video recording are prohibited without consent.
22. No liability is assumed for the loss of items, shoes, or valuables.
