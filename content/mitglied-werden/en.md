# Become a member

Anyone aged 18 or over who supports the association's purpose may join the {{orgName}} (§ 4 of the Satzung). Legal entities may be admitted on application.

## How it works

1. Submit an application in text form (letter or email) to the board, with the details needed for membership administration (§ 2 of the Geschäftsordnung).
2. The board decides, usually within six weeks. There is no right to admission, and a refusal does not need to be justified.
3. On admission, you accept the Satzung and the Geschäftsordnung.

To apply, write to <{{contactEmail}}>.

## Fees

## Volunteer hours

Each member contributes 12 hours of volunteer work per financial year, starting with the first full year after admission. Unworked hours may instead be settled with a payment of €5 per hour (§ 4 of the Geschäftsordnung).

Exemptions include members with a disability rating (GdB) of at least 50, members unable to volunteer due to illness, age or distance, documented hardship cases, and honorary members.
